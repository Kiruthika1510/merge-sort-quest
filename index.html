🧠 Merge Sort — The Core Idea

Merge Sort repeatedly divides an array into smaller halves until the pieces are trivially sorted, then merges the sorted pieces back together.

DivideSplit around the midpoint.
ConquerSort each smaller half.
CombineMerge the sorted halves.

Time: O(n log n)   •   Extra space: O(n)
